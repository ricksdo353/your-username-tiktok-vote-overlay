Narration script - paste into CapCut (Text > Text to speech) or record it yourself

[INTRO] You have three seconds to choose. And the first one... is a trap.
[Q1] Would you rather $1,000,000 right now... or $1 that doubles every day for 30 days?
[Q2] Would you rather Never use your phone again... or Never eat your favorite food again?
[Q3] Would you rather Know WHEN you'll die... or Know HOW you'll die?
[Q4] Would you rather Be able to fly... or Be invisible?
[Q5] Would you rather An AI that does all your work forever... or $50,000 cash today?
[Q6] Would you rather Go viral once... or Be rich but nobody knows you?
[Q7] Final one. The hardest. Live without music... or Live without the internet?
[END] If you took the million, you lost. One dollar doubled for thirty days is over five hundred thirty six million dollars. Comment your answers!
